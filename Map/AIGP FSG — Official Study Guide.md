## Official Sample Questions

### Q1: Explainability
An AI system is explainable when the system's input, output, and functioning are?

- **A.** Understandable to human beings. ✅
- **B.** Disclosed to human beings.
- **C.** Designed by human beings.
- **D.** Documented by human beings.

> **Answer: A** — Explainability means the system's workings are *understandable* to humans, not merely disclosed or documented.

### Q2: AI Impact Assessment
The primary purpose of an AI impact assessment is to provide understanding of?

- **A.** The return on investment of building and operating an AI system.
- **B.** The potential benefits and harms from an AI system. ✅
- **C.** The computing power necessary to run an AI system.
- **D.** The design components of an AI system.

> **Answer: B** — Impact assessments surface potential harms, benefits, and risks before deployment.

### Q3: Production Monitoring Metrics (Multi-Select)
Which of the following metrics should be tracked when monitoring the performance of a model that has been deployed into production? *(Select 3 of 5)*

- **A.** Data drift. ✅
- **B.** Data quality. ✅
- **C.** Historical training data.
- **D.** Internal training processes.
- **E.** Latency and computational metrics. ✅

> **Answer: A, B, E** — Production monitoring focuses on live system behavior (drift, quality, latency), not historical/internal processes.
